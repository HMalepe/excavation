# Teacher guide: running The Excavation with your class

For LO teachers. About two pages. You don't need to be technical.

## What it is

A self-discovery tool for Grade 9 learners choosing Grade 10 subjects (and a check-in for Grade 11). Learners answer
questions about what they like, what matters to them and how they work, try short skill checks and tasters, and talk with
the **Guide**, a computer assistant that asks follow-up questions. They get their own report. It is **not an exam** and there
are no marks.

## Before the day

1. Get the login slips from your school admin (school code + each learner's own code).
2. Check every learner on your list has **consent on file**. Learners without consent cannot log in.
3. Book a room with working devices and Wi-Fi. Phones work. Earphones help learners who use "Listen".
4. Try one login yourself.

## On the day (say this to learners)

- "This isn't a test. There are no right answers."
- "Your answers are private. Your teacher only sees a summary if **you** agree to share it."
- "The Guide is a computer program. A real person at the Excavation checks reports before you see them."
- "If you write about something that worries you, or about being unsafe, a trusted adult at school will be told so they can help. That's not a punishment."

Learners log in at the website with the **school code** and **their code** and go through the steps. Progress saves after every answer, so a freeze or a bell isn't a problem: they log in again and carry on. **Listen** reads each question aloud on their device; **Slower** slows it.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Lost code | Look it up in your school admin page |
| Learner stuck or frozen | Refresh; they continue where they left off |
| Learner upset | Stop the activity for them, talk to them privately, follow your school's child-protection procedure |
| Learner discloses abuse or danger | **Follow your school's safeguarding procedure at once.** The tool also alerts your school's safeguarding contact, but never rely on it alone |
| Parent asks about their child's data or wants it removed | Take their name and number, tell your school admin; the Excavation handles it (privacy requests) |

## Safeguarding alerts

If the Guide notices a learner may be in danger, **your school's safeguarding contact** gets an alert (email and on their dashboard). They must **confirm** it on the dashboard. If nobody confirms within **4 school hours**, it escalates to the Excavation's safeguarding lead, who will phone the school. Helplines for learners are shown inside the app.

## What you can see

- **Class progress**: who has finished which part. Not their answers.
- **Teacher summary**: only for learners who agreed to share. A short, plain-language picture to help your subject-choice conversation.
- You never see the Guide conversation unless a safeguarding alert requires it, and every such view is logged.

## After

Reports are released after a review (a few school days). Learners can download a PDF and choose to share with their teacher or family. Please don't present results as a prediction or a decision: they are a conversation starter.
