# 01 — Product Architecture
## The Excavation × Know Thy Self — School Edition (v1.0)

---

## 1. The one-line idea

**Know Thy Self measures. The Excavation interprets.**

A learner answers a scored questionnaire built on established psychology, so we have numbers that can be compared over time. Then they have a conversation with The Excavation, which finds what the numbers can't see: what makes time disappear, what drains them, whose plan they're living, what they'd choose if nobody would be disappointed. The report combines both.

We do it twice: once in **Grade 9**, when subjects are chosen, and again in **Grade 11**, when the choice can still be corrected and university applications are about to use the marks.

---

## 2. Why blend instead of running two products

| | The Excavation alone | Know Thy Self alone | Blended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depth and the feeling of being heard | Very strong | Medium | Very strong |
| Numbers you can compare across years | None | Strong | Strong |
| Recognisable to education officials | Weak ("AI conversation") | Medium | Strong (named frameworks + interpretation) |
| Time needed in a school day | 5–8 hours (adult) | 20–40 min per door | ~90 min total |
| AI cost per learner | High | Low | Medium (scores do the heavy lifting) |
| Shows change from Grade 9 to Grade 11 | Subjective only | Yes | Yes, with explanation |

The scored layer also makes the conversation faster and sharper: the AI arrives already knowing the learner's interest code, identity status and values, so it spends its questions on the things only a conversation can reveal.

---

## 3. What goes into the school edition (and what stays out)

Know Thy Self has 12 doors. For under-18s in a school setting we keep the doors that help with subject and career decisions and leave out anything clinical or sensitive.

| Know Thy Self door | School edition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mind (cognition) | **Kept** as Mindset + Study habits | Directly drives the "Rescue" verdict in Grade 11 |
| Drive (motivation) | **Kept** as Why I Do Schoolwork | Separates "I love this" from "I'm scared not to" |
| Values | **Kept** as a 10-card sort | Fast, engaging, explains career pull |
| Identity | **Kept** as Identity Status (Marcia) | Built for adolescents; measures "whose plan is it" |
| Creativity & Flow | **Kept** via the conversation layer | Flow states are best captured in conversation |
| Emotion (EQ) | **Light** — Stress Sensitivity scale only | Useful for study planning; no clinical depth |
| *(new)* Career Interests | **Added** — RIASEC | Missing from the 12 doors; the core of subject choice |
| *(new)* Personality | **Added** — Big Five (original items) | Stable backbone for comparison |
| Trauma | **Excluded** | Clinical territory; consent and liability risk with minors |
| Shadow (Dark Triad) | **Excluded** | Scoring 15-year-olds on manipulation/psychopathy ends school deals |
| Body (somatic) | **Excluded** | Not needed for the decision; adds risk |
| Senses (sensory processing) | **Excluded** | Borders on clinical assessment (OT territory) |
| Relating (attachment) | **Excluded** | Family-relationship profiling of minors is sensitive |
| Growth (developmental stage) | **Excluded** | Identity Status covers what we need |

These excluded doors stay in the adult Know Thy Self consumer product.

**Engagement mechanics for minors:** keep the cinematic reveal, the "descent" progress bar, the narrator voice and the "see you in Grade 11" hook. Remove variable-ratio rewards, sunk-cost pressure and anything designed to create compulsion. Schools and the department will look for exactly this.

## 4. The two touchpoints

### Touchpoint 1 — Grade 9 Foundation
**When:** Term 2 or early Term 3 of Grade 9, at least 4 weeks before the school's subject-choice deadline.
**Length:** about 90 minutes, which is two school periods or one long session.

| Step | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Consent check (guardian consent on file + learner agrees on screen) | 2 min |
| 1 | Intake: 3 short questions about their world | 5 min |
| 2 | Scored layer: 114 items + 2 attention checks (file 02) | 20–25 min |
| 3 | Academic snapshot: latest term marks (school import preferred) | 2 min |
| 4 | Conversation layer: 20 questions, 3 generated from their scores (file 03) | 45–60 min |
| 5 | Report generated and released (file 07, file 08) | instant + review |

**Output:** the Foundation Report: who they are, how they work, 3 subject combinations that fit, 2 to treat with caution, which university doors each choice keeps open or closes, one pattern to watch, one move to make this week. Their scores are saved as the **baseline**.

### Touchpoint 2 — Grade 11 Confirmation
**When:** Term 1 of Grade 11 (ideally weeks 3–6). Early enough to change a subject if the school allows; early enough to fix marks before the Grade 11 finals that universities use for provisional offers.
**Length:** about 75 minutes.

| Step | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Consent refresh | 2 min |
| 1 | Re-read the Grade 9 headline (shown to them) | 3 min |
| 2 | Same scored layer, identical items | 20–25 min |
| 3 | Subject Experience: 6 items per FET subject | 8 min |
| 4 | Failure-cause check: only for subjects below 50% | 0–5 min |
| 5 | Marks: Grade 10 final + Grade 11 latest term | 2 min |
| 6 | Conversation layer: 15 questions (file 05) | 30–40 min |
| 7 | Report generated with a verdict (file 06) | instant + review |

**Output:** the Confirmation Report: what stayed and what moved since Grade 9, an honest reading of each subject, one of five verdicts (Confirm, Rescue, Rethink the career, Adjust one subject, Redirect), the pass type and APS they're tracking towards, and a dated action plan.

---

## 5. The verdict model (summary — full logic in file 06)

|  | **Marks adequate** | **Marks at risk** |
|---|---|---|
| **Subjects fit them** | **CONFIRM** — map the route, raise the APS | **RESCUE** — don't switch; fix how they study and get support |
| **Subjects don't fit** | **RETHINK** — good at it but drained; keep subjects, change the career target | **REDIRECT** — switch while the school still allows it |

Plus **ADJUST ONE SUBJECT** when a single elective sits in the wrong quadrant and the rest confirm.

"Failing" never automatically means "wrong subject". The failure-cause check tells the learner which problem they actually have.

## 6. Who sees what

| Audience | What they receive | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Learner | Full report, scores in plain language, conversation transcript | Always |
| Parent/guardian | Parent summary (file 08) | Learner chooses to share; or school policy requires summary-level for minors (decide with attorney) |
| LO teacher / subject advisor | Teacher one-pager: strengths, recommended combinations, readiness flags, support needs | Learner ticks consent per report |
| School leadership | Cohort aggregates only (minimum group size 10) | Partnership agreement |
| Department / research partner | Anonymised, aggregated outcome data | Partnership agreement + guardian research consent |
| Holiday / The Excavation | Anonymised data for norming and improvement | Consent at onboarding |

Conversation content is never shown to teachers, parents or the school without the learner's explicit consent. Safeguarding disclosures are the one exception, handled by the protocol in file 09.

---

## 7. Language and access

- Voice input on by default; many learners speak better than they type.
- The learner may answer in any language. The AI replies in English unless the learner chooses their home language. **Pilot in English first** and test isiZulu, Sesotho and Setswana quality before offering them officially.
- Low-data mode: text only, no animations, images compressed. A full Grade 9 session should run under 5 MB.
- Works on the school lab computers and on learners' phones.
- Reading level target for all learner-facing text: Grade 7.

## 9. Why this matters commercially

1. **Two payments per learner**, and the bundle collects both up front.
2. **The Grade 11 report is the proof.** Same learner, measured twice, so you can show whether the Grade 9 advice held up. No aptitude test in South Africa does this.
3. **The data compounds.** After the first full cycle you have South African norms for teenagers, which makes every report more accurate and the product harder to copy.
4. **Every school renews automatically**, because there's a new Grade 9 and a new Grade 11 class every year.

5. Grade 11 Confirmation + verdict engine (files 05, 06) — needed by Term 1 of the following year at the latest.
6. Norming after ~300 learners; switch report wording from `pre_norm` to `emerging_norm`.
